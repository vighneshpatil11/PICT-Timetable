Installing on Android

The app isn't on the Play Store, so Android will ask you to allow the install once.

Download the APK

Tap Download above. If Chrome warns about the file, choose Download anyway.

Open the file

Tap the downloaded file. When asked, allow Chrome (or your Files app) to install apps.

Tap Install

Open the app and enter your roll number.

Seeing "App not installed"? Uninstall any older copy first, then try again.

Questions

 

Is it safe?

Yes. The app has no account or login. Only download it from this page or the GitHub Releases page. The file's SHA-256 checksum is listed below so you can verify it.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It's a small student-made utility shared directly. Releases on GitHub keep it free and quick to update.

Is this an official PICT app?

No. It's an individual creation and is not affiliated with PICT in any manner. It's just a utility tool.

Does it work on Android 10?

Yes. It supports Android 8.0 and above, and has been tested on Android 10.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. It's Android only.

Does it need internet?

No. Your timetable is on your phone and the PDF is read on the device. Internet is only needed to download updates or open links.

My class or room looks wrong. What do I do?

Check your roll number in Settings first. If it's right, use Feedback inside the app or message me below and I'll fix it.

How do I update?

Download the newest APK from this page and install it over the old one. Your settings stay.
